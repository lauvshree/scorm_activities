Phishing Awareness

Associate degree module (ages 18–21) — Recognize and avoid phishing tactics

Quick Guide

  • Pause when a message feels urgent or threatening.
  • Check the sender (address and domain) and hover links to preview the destination.
  • Go direct: type the official URL yourself or use the official app.
  • Never share passwords via email, chat, or forms sent to you.
  • Report anything suspicious to IT/security.
